Ultrasound AI for brachytherapy

See clearly. Treat in one room.

Mirthena is building AI that automatically identifies anatomy and brachytherapy devices on ultrasound, so cervical cancer patients can be planned and treated in a single room.

What we do

Machine learning that reads the ultrasound for you

Ultrasound is affordable, portable and already in the treatment room. Our software turns it into clear, consistent guidance during brachytherapy.

Identify anatomy

Automatically label and outline the cervix, uterus, bladder and rectum in real time.

Locate devices

Find the tandem and interstitial needles and support confident placement.

Plan in one room

Build a 3D picture for dose planning without moving the patient to MRI or CT.

See the difference

From a grey image to a guided view

Reading pelvic ultrasound takes years of experience. Our models highlight what matters, so every clinician sees the same structures.
