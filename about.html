About Mirthena

Bringing clear guidance to every brachytherapy room

Our mission is to make image-guided brachytherapy simpler, faster and available to more patients by pairing affordable ultrasound with machine learning.

The company

Ultrasound AI, starting with cervical cancer

Mirthena develops software that automatically identifies anatomy and brachytherapy devices on ultrasound images, supporting a complete single-room workflow. We are starting with cervical cancer, where our clinical expertise and the global need are greatest, and our patent-pending imaging approach is designed to extend to other ultrasound-guided procedures.

Leadership

Our founders

Onyinye “Onyi” Balogun, MD, MSc

Chief Executive Officer & Co-Founder
Harvard University · Yale School of Medicine

Dr. Balogun is a radiation oncologist with 11 years of clinical experience and a second-time founder. She is Director of Radiation Oncology Research for a hospital network, with a sustained focus on brachytherapy and cervical and prostate cancer. She was an inaugural member of the Dartmouth 3D Scholars program for physician-innovators. Mirthena grew out of the clinical need she sees in practice.

Anderson “Andy” Nnewihe, PhD, MS

Chief Technology Officer & Co-Founder
MIT · Stanford Bioengineering

Dr. Nnewihe is a medical technology leader. As a Stanford graduate student he developed a patented 18-channel breast MRI coil licensed to GE Healthcare, Philips and Siemens. Over seven years at Johnson & Johnson he directed an algorithm-based lung cancer risk initiative and led pulmonary hypertension programs built on echocardiography and ECG analytics. He previously led technology diligence at Samsung and is a Partner at Build 2 Launch, where he builds AI-driven solutions.

Our story

Opponents in 2000. Teammates today.

Onyi and Andy met at MIT in 2000 through the MITES summer program, where they entered the same entrepreneurship competition on opposing teams.

More than two decades later, a radiation oncologist and an imaging engineer are on the same team, building Mirthena together.
